# GoFurry Code of Conduct

GoFurry welcomes participants with different backgrounds, identities, abilities,
and experience levels, including developers, users, artists, and furry community
members. Everyone deserves respectful participation.

## How we participate

- Discuss code, design, facts, and ideas rather than attacking people.
- Give constructive feedback and make room for questions and disagreement.
- Respect personal boundaries, consent, privacy, and requests to stop contact.
- Keep content relevant and respect the context and rules of each project space.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, stalking, and targeted abuse.
- Publishing private information without permission or encouraging others to do so.
- Using Issues or Discussions for public shaming, dogpiling, or retaliatory abuse.
- Unwanted sexual attention or other violations of personal boundaries.

The project's furry and adult-content context does not excuse harassment or
boundary violations. Participation is not consent to unwanted content or contact.

## Moderation

This code applies in project Issues, Discussions, PRs, and other project spaces.
Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove violating content and restrict
participation where necessary. They will consider context and the safety of
participants when deciding how to respond.
